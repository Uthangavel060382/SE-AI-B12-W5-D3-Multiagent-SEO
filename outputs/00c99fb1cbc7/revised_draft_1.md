## Understanding Generative AI and Tabular AI in the SAP Ecosystem

### What Is Generative AI and How Does It Apply to SAP ERP?

Generative AI refers to artificial intelligence models that create new content or insights based on existing data patterns. Unlike traditional AI, which primarily focuses on classifying data or making predictions, generative AI can produce text, code, or data-driven recommendations—helping automate complex decisions and tasks.

Within SAP ERP, generative AI enhances key processes such as resource planning, demand forecasting, and workflow automation. By analyzing large volumes of structured ERP data, these AI models generate actionable insights that reduce manual workloads and improve operational responsiveness.

### The Role of Tabular AI in SAP

At the core of SAP’s AI innovation is **tabular AI**, a special type of AI designed to analyze tabular data—structured rows and columns typical in ERP databases. Unlike AI models built for unstructured data like text or images, tabular AI excels at working with the organized datasets commonly found in finance, procurement, manufacturing, and logistics.

Tabular AI enables smooth integration of AI directly within SAP ERP modules, powering intelligent automation and advanced predictive analytics without the need for extensive custom development. SAP’s focus on tabular AI delivers AI solutions tailored specifically to complex ERP data environments.
