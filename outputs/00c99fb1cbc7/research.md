# SEO Research Report: Generative AI in SAP ERP

## Research Summary
Recent industry coverage highlights increasing integration of generative AI with SAP ERP systems, particularly emphasizing SAP's move to embed advanced AI capabilities natively into ERP platforms. Key developments include SAP’s focus on "tabular AI" as part of their autonomous enterprise strategy, which competes on both AI model performance and integration of ERP and supply chain data. Case studies such as AMOREPACIFIC’s use of generative AI to improve SAP ECC operations demonstrate practical applications of this technology. The shift to SAP S/4HANA Cloud is also notable, with organizations like DACHSER migrating to maximize AI-driven capabilities. Overall, generative AI is becoming a core driver of transformation and efficiency in SAP ERP environments.
