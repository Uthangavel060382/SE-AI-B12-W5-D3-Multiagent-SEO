# SEO Research Report: Generative AI in SAP ERP

## Research Summary
Recent sources highlight that Generative AI is becoming a key aspect in the evolution of SAP ERP systems. SAP is integrating generative and tabular AI to enhance its ERP and supply chain offerings, positioning itself to meet the increasing demand from decision-makers expecting AI capabilities across their enterprise applications. Use cases include cloud integration, AI-driven code generation within SAP systems, and enhancing efficiency and automation in ERP operations. Leading companies such as AMOREPACIFIC and DACHSER are pioneers in deploying AI-enhanced SAP ERP solutions to transform workflows and migrate to AI-powered cloud environments.
